> **Target:** **1 QRBuddy Passport sale per day** = **A$49/day (`~$32

How do you reliably engineer **1 sale every 24 hours** without turning into a
full-time TikTok influencer? It comes down to **3 simple funnel equations**:

### 1. The Conversion Funnel Math (Why High-Intent Traffic Beats Random Viral Traffic)

Not all visitors are equal. Someone making a free Wi-Fi code for their living
room converts at `0.1%`. Someone searching _"editable QR code for restaurant
menu without subscription"_ or _"Bitly QR code alternative"_ converts at
**`2% – 4%`** because they are _already holding a credit card_ and angry at a
$35/mo bill.

To get **1 sale/day (`A$49`)**, you only need **ONE** of these daily traffic
mixes:

- **Path A (High-Intent Search / Comparison Traffic @ 2.5% conversion):**
  - Just **40 high-intent visitors/day** (`1,200/month`) landing on
    `/for/menus`, `/for/weddings`, `/for/music`, `/guide/printing`, or a
    dedicated **"Kill Your $35/mo QR Bill"** comparison/affiliate page → **4–6
    clicks on `Passport 🛂`** → **1 sale/day**.
- **Path B (Mixed Organic + Built-In Viral Pill Traffic @ 0.8% blended
  conversion):**
  - **125 total studio users/day** (`3,750/month`) creating QRs/drops → ~15% hit
    a power-user ceiling (wanting >5 dynamic links, >50 MB mixtape/photo drop,
    Breakfast/Dinner menu routing, 90-day Field Report CSV, or **White-Label
    removal of the `⚡ Made with QRBuddy` pill**) → **18 power users/day see the
    Passport card** → **~5.5% of those buy** = **1 sale/day**.

### 2. Paid Acquisition Math (Should You Spend Money on Ads? How Much?)

Because a QRBuddy Passport is a **A$49 (`~$32
USD`) one-time upfront payment** (not a $3/month trickle), your **Break-Even Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** is **A$47 (`~$31 USD`)**. To keep a healthy **3x Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** (spending A$16
to make A$49, pocketing **A$33 pure profit per sale**), here is the exact math:

- **Target Cost Per Sale (CPA):** **≤ A$16 (`~$10.50 USD`)**
- **Required Landing-Page Conversion Rate:** **2.5%** (1 in 40 clicks buys)
- **Max Cost Per Click (CPC) You Can Afford:**
  **`A$16 × 2.5% = A$0.40 ($0.26 USD) per click`**

**What this math tells you immediately:**

1. **DO NOT buy generic English Google Search ads for `"qr code generator"`:**
   - Bitly, Adobe, and Canva bid **$2.50 – $6.00 USD per click** on generic
     `"qr code generator"`. At
     $3.00/click and 2.5% conversion, 40 clicks cost **$120 USD to make $32
     USD** (you lose $88 per sale).
2. **WHERE Paid Spend ACTUALLY Prints Money ($0.08 – $0.35 CPC):**
   - **Lane 1 — Competitor & "Anti-Subscription" Long-Tail Search
     (`$0.20 – $0.45 CPC`):**
     - Exact-match phrases: `"qr code generator no monthly fee"`,
       `"one time payment dynamic qr code"`, `"bitly qr code too expensive"`,
       `"editable qr code for restaurant menu"`.
   - **Lane 2 — Spanish / LatAm & Mexico Meta/Google Ads
     (`$0.04 – $0.15 USD CPC`):**
     - English CPCs are 5–10x more expensive than Spanish CPCs, yet
       Mexican/LatAm restaurants, wedding planners, DJs, and agencies face the
       exact same $600 MXN/mo Bitly hostage problem — and
       **`https://qrbuddy.app/es` is 100% native Buena Onda Spanish**.
   - **Lane 3 — Micro-Sponsorships & Affiliate Comparison Pages (`$0 upfront` or
     fixed `$50` newsletter slot):**
     - Instead of paying Google per click, partner with indie
       restaurant/hospitality newsletters, wedding-planning blogs, band/synth
       communities, and "alternative-to" directories on a **30%–40% affiliate
       split** (`~$10–$13 USD` per sale, paid _only_ when a sale actually
       happens — zero risk!).
